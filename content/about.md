## About Solar Planner Guide

Solar Planner Guide helps homeowners decide whether solar makes sense for them: costs, incentives, financing, contracts and choosing an installer.

## Who writes here

Articles are published under pen names, each covering one part of the site:

- **[Alex Rivera](/alex-rivera/)**: solar system sizing and design
- **[Craig Stevens](/craig-stevens/)**: solar loans, leases, power purchase agreements and tax credits
- **[Derek Hansen](/derek-hansen/)**: installation, permitting and utility interconnection
- **[Nadia Patel](/nadia-patel/)**: solar alongside home electrification and household energy use
- **[Patricia Moore](/patricia-moore/)**: incentives, net metering and state solar policy

## How we work

Articles are drafted with AI writing tools from public sources and pass an automated accuracy check before they are published. The full process, the sources we rely on and the limits of what this site can tell you are in our [editorial standards](/editorial-standards/).

## Important limits

Incentives, net-metering rules and utility rates change, sometimes mid-year, and they differ by state and utility. Our savings figures and calculators are estimates. Get written quotes from more than one licensed installer, read any loan, lease or power purchase agreement in full, and confirm incentives with your utility and state energy office before you sign.
